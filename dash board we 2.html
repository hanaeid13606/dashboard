▥ Company Name & Type

Telecom Egypt (WE)

Public joint-stock telecommunications company listed on the Egyptian Exchange (EGX: ETEL).

◷ History

Egypt’s telecommunications history dates to 1854. Telecom Egypt was established in its modern corporate form in 1998; the WE brand launched in 2017.

⌖ Headquarters

Cairo / Smart Village, Giza, Egypt

Smart Village is a major corporate location.

⚙ Main Business Activities
  • Mobile services

  • Digital services & ICT solutions
  • Wholesale and international connectivity
  • Cloud, data centers & infrastructure

ⓘ   Financial Q&A Analysis

Key interpretations for the graduation project
Q1–Q3 • Income & FX

Did revenue growth translate into profit?

Revenue grew over the period, but net profit fluctuated due to foreign-currency debt and exchange-rate effects. The supplied project notes cite EGP 19.7bn in finance losses in 2024 and a stronger 2025 profit contribution from Vodafone Egypt.

Q4–Q6 • Balance sheet

Leverage and asset structure

Borrowings grew approximately 4.4× while equity changed relatively little. Property and equipment account for about 50% of assets, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of telecommunications networks.

Liquidity • Q7

What do liquidity ratios show?

Current and quick ratios below 1.0 suggest current liabilities exceed current assets and the company may face short-term liquidity pressure. Interpret this alongside operating cash flow and debt maturities.

Q8–Q12 • Returns

Why can ROE be much higher than ROA?

ROE measures profit relative to shareholders’ equity; ROA measures profit relative to total assets. Debt-financed assets can magnify ROE, but also increase financial risk. A high ROE alone does not prove stronger operating performance.

Q13 • EBITDA & FX

How does FX affect reported EBITDA?

Foreign-exchange effects can materially affect reported results, depending on where gains and losses are classified. Compare reported EBITDA with EBITDA excluding FX and check the company’s exact reconciliation in its annual report.
