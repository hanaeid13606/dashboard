Key Takeaways
ROA (12.1% in 2025): Measures net profit relative to total assets. Demonstrates how efficiently management utilizes the EGP 213.4bn asset base.
ROE (63.6% in 2025): Measures return generated on shareholders' equity. Amplified significantly by high debt financing of total assets.
Leverage Effect: While high leverage magnifies returns when profits surge, it also exposes the company to extreme volatility during currency devaluations.

Interactive summary answering core questions on income, leverage, liquidity, and profitability.

Q1-Q3: Income & FX Impact

Did revenue growth translate to profit?

Revenue grew every year, but net profit fluctuated wildly due to foreign currency debt losses (e.g., EGP 19.7bn finance loss in 2024). In 2025, currency losses ceased, and profit rebounded strongly with Vodafone Egypt profit share doubling to EGP 14.3bn.

Q4-Q6: Balance Sheet & Leverage

Leverage and Asset Structure

The company became significantly more leveraged. Borrowings grew ~4.4x while equity barely changed. Property and equipment account for ~50% of assets, reflecting heavy network investments, alongside a 14% stake in Vodafone Egypt.

Q7-Q13: Liquidity & ROE/ROA

Liquidity & High ROE Drivers

Liquidity is weak (Current ratio < 1). However, 2025 ROE reached 63.6% compared to ROA of 12.1%, showing that liabilities heavily finance assets, magnifying returns while increasing risk. EBITDA margin excluding FX reached 54.1% in 2025.
